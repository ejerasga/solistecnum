Get the facts about going solar with Solis Tectum. Can't find your answer? Chat with our AI assistant or contact us directly.

Most homeowners see a 40–70% reduction in their monthly electric bills after switching to solar. For instance, a ₱10,000/month bill can drop down significantly to ₱3,000–₱6,000 depending on your daily daytime consumption, system capacity, and roof orientation.

A typical residential installation takes 1–3 days depending on system size and roof complexity. The full project timeline from initial site assessment to system design and final commissioning is typically completed smoothly within 1–2 weeks.

A Grid-Tied system powers your home during daylight hours directly from the sun, drastically reducing your utility bill with minimal equipment cost. A Hybrid system includes dedicated lithium energy storage (batteries), ensuring your critical home appliances keep running smoothly even during power outages or brownouts.

We use high-grade Tier-1 monocrystalline panels and industry-recognized smart inverters (such as Deye, Growatt, and Solis). These components offer superior solar conversion efficiency, durable weather resistance, and dependable performance engineered for Philippine rooftop conditions.
